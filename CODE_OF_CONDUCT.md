# Code of conduct

This project is used by people who put real money behind it, and it is built by people from many
places and backgrounds. Both deserve the same thing: plain, respectful and honest discussion.

## What we expect

- Talk about the code, the numbers and the evidence, not about the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you conclude.
- Be precise about what you tested and what you did not. A claim about trading results comes with
  the records behind it.
- Welcome questions from people who are new to trading or to the code.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, keys or account details.
- Promoting a token, a signal service or an investment, or asking others to fund one.
- Repeatedly pushing a change after maintainers have explained why it was declined.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block participants
who do not follow this code. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting on this repository and say it is a conduct report; it reaches the maintainers only.

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any project space where
someone represents the project.
